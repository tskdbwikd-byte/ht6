# YoursPrinted Branding

Purpose: approachable, student-focused, community 3D printing service — friendly, efficient, and affordable.

Colors
- Primary: Teal  (HEX: #0EA5A2)
- Accent: Dark Slate (HEX: #0F172A)
- Neutral light: Off-white (HEX: #F7F7F8)
- Warning: Orange (HEX: #FB923C)
- Success: Green (HEX: #10B981)

Typography
- Headings: Inter or system sans-serif
- Body: Inter / system sans-serif

Logo
- Use `frontend/public/logo.svg` as a placeholder. Replace with a full SVG mark when available.

Tone & Copy
- Friendly, clear, concise.
- Examples: "Upload your file", "Estimate price", "Fast community prints"

Imagery
- Use photos of campus makerspaces, students, and prints. Keep images high-contrast and natural.

Iconography
- Simple geometric icons for file, price tag, queue, and profile.

Spacing & Layout
- Use comfortable spacing (8–24px), narrow centered content width (~720px) for forms and descriptions.
